# Simple Business Model: $2,500/Year per Engineer

## 💰 **Pricing Strategy**

### **Target Price: $2,500/year per engineer**
- **Monthly equivalent**: ~$208/month
- **Break-even**: 50 hours saved per year
- **ROI for customer**: 100%+ (saves $5,000, costs $2,500)

### **Why This Price Works:**
- **Low enough** - Engineers can expense without approval
- **High enough** - Covers development and support costs
- **Simple** - No complex tiers or usage limits
- **Recurring** - Annual subscription model

---

## 🎯 **Target Customer**

### **Primary Customer: Facility Engineer**
- **Salary**: $50-100/hour
- **Responsibility**: 50-500+ equipment units
- **Pain point**: Writing repetitive procedures
- **Budget**: Can expense $2,500/year easily

### **Secondary Customer: Engineering Manager**
- **Team size**: 5-20 engineers
- **Budget**: $12,500-50,000/year for team
- **Value**: Standardized procedures across team

---

## 🚀 **Customer Acquisition Strategy**

### **1. Direct Sales to Engineers**
- **LinkedIn outreach** - Target facility engineers
- **Industry forums** - Data center and construction communities
- **Trade shows** - DatacenterDynamics, BICSI, etc.
- **Cold email** - Personalized to their specific equipment

### **2. Content Marketing**
- **Case studies** - Show time savings
- **Demo videos** - 5-minute walkthrough
- **Blog posts** - "How to automate procedure writing"
- **Webinars** - Live demonstrations

### **3. Referral Program**
- **$500 credit** for successful referrals
- **Engineers know other engineers** - Word of mouth is powerful
- **Industry is tight-knit** - Good reputation spreads fast

## 🛠️ **Product Features for $2,500/Year**

### **Core Features:**
- **Database connectivity** - Connect to existing equipment databases
- **Template library** - CRAH, UPS, PDU, CRAC procedures
- **AI mapping** - Smart suggestions for data mapping
- **Export options** - Word, PDF, Excel output
- **Cloud storage** - Save and sync procedures

### **Advanced Features:**
- **Custom templates** - Create your own procedure formats
- **Team sharing** - Share templates with colleagues
- **Version control** - Track changes and updates
- **Compliance checking** - Ensure procedures meet standards
- **Mobile access** - View procedures on mobile devices
